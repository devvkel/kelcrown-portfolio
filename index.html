launch copy · Web3

Dry Run: launch copy for my own product

I built Dry Run, a free tool that lets creators rehearse a vibe/vibe token launch on Robinhood Chain testnet before their settings lock forever. Then I wrote the whole launch: the product tweet, the founder quote post and the community posts.

  • Hook-first launch tweet with a short demo video
  • Founder quote post from my main account to add a personal angle
  • Community launch posts and a share card people could post with their own results

reply strategy · paid role

SNVR: daily reply work

I’m paid to handle replies for SNVR. It’s a reply-only role: every day I write thoughtful replies under the right posts so new people see the project and join the conversation.

  • Pick the conversations worth joining, not just the biggest ones
  • Write replies that add something, never generic “gm, great project”
  • Keep the project’s voice the same across every reply
